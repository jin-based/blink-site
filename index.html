What it does

A status light with a personality — and a shelf.

Blink communicates through the notch, so you learn things at a glance and never have to open anything.

Lives in the notch

Blink tucks into the edge of the notch and peeks out when you hover — showing what it's holding without taking focus or covering your menu bar. It sleeps over full-screen apps, during a Focus, and while you present. Hover wakes it.

Stash

Drop files, text, links or images on Blink; drag them back out when you need them. Quick Look, pin, undo, and a 7-day Recently cleared. Your originals are never moved.

Now Playing

Music and Spotify, with cover-tinted sound bars in the notch. A mini player you can float on the desktop, dock beside the panel, or resize.

Little reactions

Follows the cursor, dozes late at night, hops when the charger clicks in, catches finished downloads. System events only — never what you type or which app is open.

Three themes

Notch Black grows out of the notch. Glass follows light and dark. Cozy is ice on navy with a faint room behind your things.

Yours, and only on this Mac

No accounts, no server, no analytics. Blink keeps references to your files and small copies of your clips in Application Support — nothing leaves the machine. It reads the Finder selection only when you ask, and the download folder only if you turn that on.
